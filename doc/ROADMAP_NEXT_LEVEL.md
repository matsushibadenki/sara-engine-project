## Research Focus Reset — 2026-09-05

The current research priority is a CPU-first local temporal learner with verified memory. First qualify an independent task with delayed outcomes and repeated present-state contexts. Then compare the smallest causal mechanism with strong current-state and event-driven scalar controls under matched inputs and actual local updates.
